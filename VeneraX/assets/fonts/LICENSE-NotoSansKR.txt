Bundled font: Noto Sans KR (Korean subset)
================================================================================
Files
  assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-Regular.otf   (family "Noto Sans KR", weight 400)
  assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-Bold.otf      (family "Noto Sans KR", weight 700)

Provenance
  Downloaded from the `@expo-google-fonts/noto-sans-kr@0.2.3` package on npm
  (unpkg.com), which redistributes the upstream Google Fonts binaries
  unmodified. Verified after download by reading the sfnt `name` table:
    nameID 1 = "Noto Sans KR", nameID 2 = "Regular"/"Bold",
    OS/2 usWeightClass = 400 / 700, 24869 glyphs, `OTTO` (CFF outlines).
  The binary itself embeds the same licence notice as nameID 13 (reproduced
  in the font, and quoted at the end of this file).

  Scope: **Korean (Hangul) + Hanja + Latin** -- 23176 codepoints in the cmap,
  verified by reading it: U+D55C -> glyph 20852, U+AE00 -> glyph 10776,
  U+4E2D (Hanja) -> glyph 2508. This is the face that closes the gap
  registered in docs/P9.6 section 6 item 1 (Hangul was absent from the SC
  subset). Simplified-only glyphs still come from Noto Sans SC through the
  fallback chain (`bundledFamilyFallbacks` in lib/foundation/bundled_fonts.dart)
  rather than from a platform font.
